## Yamuna Expressway Region

The Yamuna Expressway connects Greater Noida with Agra and passes through an area experiencing significant infrastructure and real estate development. The surrounding region is also gaining attention because of developments associated with the Noida International Airport and other infrastructure projects.

Property buyers should independently verify project approvals, specifications, pricing, availability and other relevant information from appropriate official sources before making a property decision.
